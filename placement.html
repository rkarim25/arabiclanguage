🎧 Listening placement اِخْتِبَار الاِسْتِمَاع

Five minutes, ears only

12 questions, all audio — no reading. Six recited verses (one ayah each) from your salah surahs, then six single words. Pick the meaning you heard. Replay as often as you like (replays are counted honestly).

This is a measurement, not a drill: the result anchors your skill chart (the ◆ marks) and recalibrates the forecast. A low score is USEFUL — it makes the chart true. Take one every week or two.
